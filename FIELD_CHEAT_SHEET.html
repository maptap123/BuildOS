Field Cheat Sheet

Crew member
01

Clock In

  1. Tap Time Clock in the bottom nav.
  2. Confirm today's job — it's pre-filled from wherever you last were.
  3. Tap Clock In. The timer starts and shows the job name.
  4. End of shift: open Time Clock again, tap Clock Out.
02

Log a Day

  1. From the job, tap Log, then Add Log.
  2. Tap Take Photo — camera opens directly, no gallery digging.
  3. Talk or type what happened. No signal? It saves on your phone and sends once you're back online.
  4. Tap Save. Look for the synced checkmark later.
03

Find the Site

  1. Open the job, tap the address at the top.
  2. Opens turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps.
  3. Need the homeowner? Tap their name to call or text — right from the job screen.
04

Ask Fixer

  1. Fixer's icon floats on every screen — tap it any time.
  2. Tap the mic and just talk, or type your question.
  3. Quick taps for common asks: "My tasks today" · "What's overdue?" · "Start a log" · "Summarize this job"
Stuck? Flag a task as blocked (the flag icon) and your PM gets notified.
